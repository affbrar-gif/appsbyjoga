Common questions

Where are my clips and photos saved?

In your Photos app, in Recents. The first time you tap SAVE, the app asks for permission to add to Photos. Please tap Allow.

How does SAVE work?

Pick a length on the main screen: 10s, 20s, 30s, 60s or 120s. When something happens, tap SAVE. The app keeps that many seconds before your tap, plus a few seconds after it (5 seconds by default). You can change the seconds after SAVE in Settings ⚙︎.

Why is my clip shorter than the length I picked?

The app can only save what it has recorded. For example, if you open the app and tap SAVE with 60s chosen after only 20 seconds, the clip is about 20 seconds (plus the seconds after). The screen shows a note when this happens.

Can I take a photo while recording?

Yes. Tap the white round button on the right. The photo is saved to Photos and the video keeps recording.

Does it fill up my storage?

No. The app only keeps the last 2 minutes while recording and deletes older video all the time. Video you did not save is deleted when you stop or leave the app. Only the clips and photos you save stay on your iPhone.

Does it record when the app is closed or the screen is locked?

No. iOS does not allow camera recording in the background. The screen stays on while the app records. When you come back to the app, recording starts again.

My clip has no sound.

Microphone permission is probably off. Go to iPhone Settings → Replay Save Cam → turn on Microphone.

The quality changed from 4K to 1080p by itself.

4K uses a lot of power and can make the iPhone warm. When the phone gets hot, the app changes to 1080p to keep recording smoothly. You can change it back in Settings ⚙︎ when the phone is cooler.

Why did the buffer restart?

Changing the camera (front/back) or the video quality starts a new buffer. A phone call or another app using the camera can also pause recording for a moment.

Does Replay Save Cam upload my videos?

No. Everything is done on your iPhone. The app has no servers and collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.

Is Replay Save Cam really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no account.
