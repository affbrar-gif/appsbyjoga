Privacy Policy

APP NAME · Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version
  • We collect no data. Not your photos, not your contacts, not your name, not your email.
  • Everything runs on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

This policy explains how the APP NAME iOS app ("APP NAME", "the app", "we") handles your information. APP NAME is made by Apps by Joga.

1. Information we collect

None. APP NAME has no servers and does not send any of your information over the internet. We cannot see your photos, videos, contacts or any other data on your device.

2. Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhyWhere the data goes
Photos To find duplicate photos, bursts, similar shots, large files, screenshots and videos, and to delete the items you choose. Scanned on your iPhone only. Never uploaded.
Contacts To find duplicate and empty contacts, and to merge, delete or restore the contacts you choose. Read and changed on your iPhone only. Never uploaded.
Face ID / Passcode (optional) To lock the Vault screen if you turn on "Face ID Lock". Handled by iOS. The app only gets a yes/no result and never sees your face data.

You can change these permissions at any time in iPhone Settings → APP NAME. If you allow "Limited Access", APP NAME only sees the photos or contacts you picked.

3. Deleting photos and contacts

APP NAME only deletes items you select. Before any photo is deleted, iOS shows its own confirmation. Deleted photos go to the "Recently Deleted" album in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for 30 days.

Before APP NAME merges or deletes contacts, it saves a backup file (.vcf) inside the app on your iPhone. You can restore these contacts from the Vault screen. Backups are deleted after 30 days if "Auto-delete backups" is on, or when you delete them yourself.

4. Data stored on your device

To work faster, APP NAME keeps a few small files inside the app on your iPhone:

  • a list of photo file sizes, so the next scan is faster;
  • a history of your clean-ups (date, number of items, space freed);
  • contact backup files (see section 3);
  • your settings (Auto-delete, Face ID Lock).

These files never leave your device, except as part of your own iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone (controlled by Apple and you). Deleting the app deletes all of these files.

APP NAME also reads your iPhone's total and free storage space, only to show it on the Overview screen.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any information, because we do not have any. The only time data leaves the app is if you choose "Export All Contacts", which opens the iOS share sheet so you can save or send the file where you want.

6. Third parties

APP NAME has no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools. Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and app usage data only if you agreed to share them with app developers in your iPhone settings. This is controlled by Apple's privacy policy.

7. Children

APP NAME does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or under 16 where that rule applies).

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You stay in full control of your data on your iPhone. This applies under the Australian Privacy Act, the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, the EU/UK GDPR and the California CCPA.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a future version of the app starts collecting any data, we will tell you in the app first.
