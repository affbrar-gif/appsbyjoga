Common questions

I deleted photos but my free space did not go up. Why?

iOS moves deleted photos to Recently Deleted and keeps them for 30 days. To free the space now, open the Photos app → Albums → Recently Deleted → Select → Delete All.

Can I undo a contact merge or delete?

Yes. APP NAME saves a backup before every change. Open the Vault tab, find the clean-up and tap Restore. Backups are kept for 30 days.

Can I get deleted photos back?

Yes, for 30 days. Open the Photos app → Albums → Recently Deleted → select the photos → Recover.

APP NAME only shows some of my photos or contacts.

You may have given "Limited Access". Go to iPhone Settings → APP NAME → Photos (or Contacts) and choose Full Access.

How does APP NAME choose the "Best" photo?

For bursts, it uses the photo your iPhone picked as the best shot when there is one. Otherwise it picks the photo with the most pixels and detail. You can always choose a different one. Hold any photo to see it full screen.

Does APP NAME upload my photos or contacts?

No. Everything is done on your iPhone. APP NAME has no servers and collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.

Is APP NAME really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no account.
