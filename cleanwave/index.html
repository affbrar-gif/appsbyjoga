CleanWave

Free up space on your iPhone. Find duplicate photos, bursts, similar shots, large videos, screenshots and duplicate contacts. Everything happens on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

What CleanWave does
  • Finds exact duplicate photos, bursts and similar shots, and suggests the best one to keep
  • Shows large photos and videos by size (5 MB+ up to 500 MB+)
  • Finds duplicate contacts and lets you choose which name and numbers to keep
  • Saves a backup on your iPhone before changing any contact, so you can undo it
