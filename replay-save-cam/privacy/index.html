Privacy Policy

Replay Save Cam · Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version
  • We collect no data. Not your videos, not your photos, not your voice, not your name, not your email.
  • Everything happens on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Video you do not save is deleted automatically.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

This policy explains how the Replay Save Cam iOS app ("Replay Save Cam", "the app", "we") handles your information. Replay Save Cam is made by Apps by Joga.

1. Information we collect

None. Replay Save Cam has no servers and does not send any of your information over the internet. We cannot see your videos, photos, sound recordings or any other data on your device.

2. Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhyWhere the data goes
Camera To show the live picture, record video and take photos. Recorded on your iPhone only. Never uploaded.
Microphone To record sound with your video clips. Recorded on your iPhone only. Never uploaded. If you say no, clips are saved without sound.
Photos (add only) To save the clips and photos you choose to keep. Saved to your Photos app. The app can add items but cannot see or read your photo library.

You can change these permissions at any time in iPhone Settings → Replay Save Cam.

3. How recording works

While the camera is on, Replay Save Cam records into a temporary buffer inside the app on your iPhone. The buffer only holds the most recent 2 minutes. Older video is deleted all the time while you record.

When you tap SAVE, the app copies the seconds you chose into your Photos app. Everything else in the buffer is deleted when you:

  • tap Stop;
  • leave the app (go to the Home Screen or switch apps);
  • close the app;
  • open the app again after it stopped unexpectedly.

The app does not record when it is not on screen. iOS does not allow camera recording in the background.

4. Data stored on your device

Apart from the temporary buffer (section 3), Replay Save Cam keeps only your settings inside the app: clip length, seconds after SAVE, video quality and which camera you used. These settings never leave your device, except as part of your own iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone (controlled by Apple and you). Deleting the app deletes them.

Clips and photos you save are stored in your Photos app. They are yours, and the app cannot see or delete them afterwards.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any information, because we do not have any. If you share a saved clip, you do it yourself from the Photos app.

6. Third parties

Replay Save Cam has no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools. Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and app usage data only if you agreed to share them with app developers in your iPhone settings. This is controlled by Apple's privacy policy.

7. Recording other people

You are responsible for using Replay Save Cam in line with the laws where you are. Some places require consent before you record people or conversations. Please respect other people's privacy.

8. Children

Replay Save Cam does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or under 16 where that rule applies).

9. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You stay in full control of your data on your iPhone. This applies under the Australian Privacy Act, the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, the EU/UK GDPR and the California CCPA.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a future version of the app starts collecting any data, we will tell you in the app first.
