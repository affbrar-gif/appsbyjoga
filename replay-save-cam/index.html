Replay Save Cam

Save moments after they happen. Keep the camera running, and when something great happens, tap SAVE. The app keeps the last few seconds and deletes the rest, so your storage stays free.

What Replay Save Cam does
  • Keeps recording, but only holds the last 2 minutes in a temporary buffer
  • Tap SAVE to keep the last 10, 20, 30, 60 or 120 seconds, plus a few seconds after the tap
  • Take photos while the video keeps recording
  • Records in up to 4K, with sound
  • Saves clips and photos straight to your Photos app. Unsaved video is deleted
